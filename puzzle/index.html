“We already run conversion lift studies.”

Good, keep running them. They're fast, free, and the right tool for tactical reads inside one platform. Then notice what they can never give you:

  • Your sales, not their conversions. A lift study is graded by the platform selling you the media, on its own modelled, consent-gated conversion data. A geo-experiment is graded on your order book.
  • Depth no platform can offer. The readout is your data, so you can cut it: is the lift different for new vs returning customers? For handbags vs shoes? Which parts of the business actually depend on paid media?

Use both, but only one is graded by someone other than the platform selling you the media.

Why Europe is the hard mode, and what we did about it

In the US, geo-testing is a solved shape: 210 DMAs, every platform speaks them. In Europe, Meta targets French cities, or régions but on 2013 boundaries, Google speaks criteria IDs that mix several geographic levels and resist clean aggregation, TikTok has provinces with English exonyms, your orders carry postal codes, and in the UK, targeting, attribution and official geography are three different maps. This is where first geo-experiments die: regions that can be targeted but not measured, or measured but not targeted. First trustworthy readout: next quarter.

We pre-paid that tax

The eDMA layer covers Europe country by country, including a fully engineered UK, with every unit verified on two criteria at once: the platforms can execute it, your sales can be attributed to it. Designs are only ever proposed in units that pass both. The classic failure mode is not warned about; it is impossible by construction. The calendar time goes into the flight, not the plumbing.

From one number to the next budget

One experiment is a verdict on one channel. The program turns verdicts into allocation:

  • Response curves, from experiments A two-cell test runs the same channel at two spend intensities and returns three points on its response curve: zero, level one, level two. That's where “spend more” stops being an opinion and saturation becomes visible.
  • Your MMM, calibrated on causal ground truth. Every readout emits a calibration row, channel × country × window × lift with its confidence interval, that anchors your Marketing Mix Model to experiments instead of noisy observational variation. Better inputs. Better calibration. Better decisions.
  • Built for marketing teams, trusted by finance. Every number traces back through an audit trail to raw model output — methodology your CFO can interrogate, outputs shaped for budget planning, not for a dashboard wall.

The science, unhidden

PuZzLe runs on Meta's open-source GeoLift — augmented synthetic control, the academic standard for geo-incrementality: a weighted blend of untouched regions reconstructs what the test regions would have done, and the difference is the lift your spend caused. We add the guard rails the paper assumes and practice forgets: mandatory placebo tests, donor buffers against spillover, cooldowns for ad carryover, and every number on screen labelled exact or estimated.
